# Product Vision

## Product

AI Lead Qualification & Outreach Operations Agent.

## Vision

Give small teams an AI sales-operations layer that turns scattered lead information into explainable priorities and useful next actions while keeping consequential actions under human control.

## Target users

- Solo founders
- Freelancers
- Small-business operators
- Sales and business-development professionals

## Core promise

Instead of manually reviewing every lead, the user can ask the system to identify the best opportunities, explain why they matter, prepare personalized outreach, and present actions for approval.

## Design principle

**AI recommends. Human approves. System executes.**

## Non-goals

The MVP is not a full CRM, autonomous mass-mailing system, payment system, or general-purpose autonomous agent.
